# Pack design and evidence

Each pack stores process vocabulary, layers, draft expectations, possible failure modes and suggested investigation steps. These are versioned design content; a draft pattern is not an empirically verified cause or approved business policy.

Guidance entries describe the expectation, why it matters, measurement, possible explanations, checks, actions, responsible roles and sources. Generic pack guidance and project-specific evidence remain separate. Use plain language before method terms.

References to this document identify the internal pack design, not independent validation. Dataset measurements and external publications retain their own source entries. Check each entry's review status and the underlying source before relying on a numerical claim. Template thresholds require an explicit calibration or policy decision.

The bundled templates, guidance and display fixtures share this reference. Changes to source metadata can change a newly imported norm's fingerprint; existing saved norm versions are not rewritten.
